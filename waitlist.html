No more awkward phone calls.

You shouldn't have to ring six clinics to find out what a filling costs. Dentists compete, you choose.

  • 1 · Snap four guided photos. On-camera guides walk you through front, upper, lower and the problem area. About 60 seconds.
  • 2 · Real dentists quote you. AHPRA-registered dentists nearby send personally-signed indicative quotes — each with the practitioner's name and registration number.
  • 3 · Choose and book. Compare on price, distance, availability and reviews. Book the consult with a fully refundable deposit.

Why you can trust it.

  • AHPRA-verified dentists only. Every quoting dentist is checked against the AHPRA public register before they can send a quote. Suspended or restricted practitioners are silently dropped.
  • Named, personally-signed quotes. The dentist's name, AHPRA number and clinic are on every quote card — timestamped and audited.
  • Deposits held by us, returned in full. Your refundable platform deposit is held by QuoteMySmile, never the clinic, and returned to your card within 5 business days of your visit.
  • Free for patients. Photos deleted after 30 days. Nothing to pay to use QuoteMySmile. Your photos and location are never sold, and auto-purged 30 days after each request closes.
