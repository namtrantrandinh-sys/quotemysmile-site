## Hero model — `model-cutout.webp`, `model-portrait.webp`

- **Source:** Pexels — https://www.pexels.com/photo/posing-woman-in-jean-jacket-16396072/
- **Photographer:** Tessy Agbonome
- **Licence:** [Pexels Licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/) — free for
  commercial use, no attribution required, modification permitted.
- **Processing:** background keyed out and the figure composited on brand mint
  by `outreach/make_hero_model.py`. Re-run that script to regenerate both files.

**Usage limits — read before repurposing.** The Pexels Licence permits commercial
use, but Pexels does **not** guarantee a model release. That means:

- Do **not** caption or imply she is a patient, a customer, or a QuoteMySmile
  user, and do not place her next to a treatment claim or an outcome statement.
  Alt text is deliberately the neutral "A person smiling".
- Do **not** present her as endorsing the service. Under the Pexels Licence,
  using an identifiable person in a way that implies endorsement is not covered.
- This matters more here than on a normal site: AHPRA's advertising rules
  restrict testimonials for regulated health services, and an implied
  patient-with-a-good-outcome image is the exact shape of what they restrict.

If the brand ever wants her framed as a patient, commission a shoot with a
signed model release instead.
